Common questions

Clear answers upfront

How are scope, timing, and pricing determined?

After an initial conversation, I prepare a proposal with the scope, timeline, and price. Everything is agreed before work begins, so you know what to expect.

Can I easily edit and update the website myself?

We agree which content you need to manage before we begin. I set up the editor or CMS for that scope and show you how to update your website at handover.

What is included with the VELORA template?

VELORA is an editable, responsive Framer landing page with customizable sections, fonts, colors, and subtle animations. Setup guidance and email support are included. The planned price is USD 39 as a one-time purchase; a Framer site plan and domain are separate. The demo is available while the template is being prepared for launch.
