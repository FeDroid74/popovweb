Privacy policy
PopovWeb · 2026-10-06

Controller and contact
Fedor Vladimirovich Popov (Попов Федор Владимирович), Russian Federation, operates PopovWeb and handles enquiries. Contact for privacy requests: fedorpopov7@yandex.ru.

Enquiry data
The form requests your name and one reply channel: a Telegram username, WhatsApp telephone number or email address. A project description is optional. The selected website format, form language and consent confirmation are also submitted. Please do not include identity documents, payment or health information, or third-party personal data.

Purpose and basis
Enquiry data is used to respond, clarify the project and discuss a website development proposal, based on the separate consent given before submission. Information required for a subsequent contract may be processed on the applicable legal basis. An enquiry does not subscribe you to advertising.

Handling and recipients
Processing includes receipt, recording, organisation, storage, correction, use for responding and deletion. When the form is connected, its server sends the name, contact, message, project format and consent information to the controller through the Telegram Bot API. Enquiries are not published on the website. Telegram is a third-party service with its own policy at https://telegram.org/privacy. Applicable Russian requirements, including data localisation and cross-border transfer requirements, apply to enquiry processing.

Retention
Enquiry data is used until the response and related discussion are completed or consent is withdrawn, unless another legal basis permits continued processing. Contract-related information is retained as required for performance and legal obligations. Data is deleted within the applicable statutory periods when the purpose ends or consent is withdrawn.

Technical data and browser storage
Hosting infrastructure may process an IP address, request time, page address and browser information to deliver pages and prevent abuse. Theme and language preferences are stored in browser localStorage. Switching language temporarily transfers a form draft through the tab’s sessionStorage; this storage entry is removed on arrival at the next language version. Unsubmitted drafts are not sent to the controller.

Yandex services
Yandex Metrica is not enabled in this version. Visitor analytics using Metrica is planned. Before activation, this policy will be updated with the actual settings, data and retention terms, and visitors will be given an analytics choice. Yandex Webmaster is planned for search visibility management; it does not by itself add a visitor tracking tag. Yandex policy: https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/.

Your requests
You can request information, correction, blocking or deletion of your data and withdraw consent by emailing fedorpopov7@yandex.ru. Include the contact used in your enquiry to help locate it, without unnecessary identity documents. Browsing the site does not require submitting the form. You may seek protection of your rights from the competent authority or a court.

Protection and updates
Enquiry access is intended for the controller and persons lawfully involved in processing. The form must use HTTPS and the bot token must remain on the server. Changes to processing will be reflected in a new policy version. Updated: 6 October 2026.
